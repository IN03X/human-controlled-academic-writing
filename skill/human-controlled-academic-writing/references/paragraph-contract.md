# Paragraph Contract

Use paragraph contracts for related work, advisor revision, and any section where prose has drifted into summary.

## Template

```markdown
| Position | Local claim | Allowed content | Forbidden content | Reader takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Section X paragraph 1 | ... | ... | ... | Readers should understand: ... |
```

## Local Claim Rules

A local claim should be concrete:

- "Cylinder scattering experiments measured responses under controlled receiver/source geometries."
- "Room-acoustic benchmarks publish reproducible room-scale inputs and impulse responses."
- "The selected frequency bands reduce the influence of low-frequency empty-field drift."

Avoid claims like:

- "This provides a foundation."
- "This shows the importance of the method."
- "This supports the framework."

## Reader Takeaway Rules

Write the takeaway as a plain sentence. The reader should not need to infer what the paragraph was for.

Bad:

- "This indicates the necessity of a unified benchmark."

Better:

- "Readers should understand that existing datasets publish raw audio and hardware information, but their response variables are organized for imaging or recognition rather than object-level directional response."
